Privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Social Fusion (“we”, “us”) is a video platform where people watch and upload videos and shorts, go live, post stories, comment and send virtual gifts. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights you have. It applies to the Social Fusion app and to socialfusion.tech. Please also read our Terms of Use and Community Guidelines.

In short

We collect only what we need to run the app. We do not sell your personal information. You control your content and permissions, and you can delete your account and data at any time (see how).

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: username, email address, password (stored in protected form), profile photo, bio and language.
  • Sign-in with Google: if you use Google Sign-In, we receive your name, email address and profile photo from Google. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Content you create: videos, shorts, live streams, stories, captions, comments, likes, subscriptions, playlists and messages.
  • Wallet and payouts: coins, gifts, earnings and withdrawal requests, plus the payout details you provide. Payments are handled by our payment partners, and we do not store your card or bank login details.
  • Reports and support: the reports you send and your emails to us.

Information we collect while you use the app

  • Device and technical data: device model, operating system, app version, language, IP address, crash reports and error logs.
  • Usage data: what you watch, search for, like and share, and how long you watch. We use this to recommend videos and improve the app.
  • Advertising data: your device advertising ID and your interactions with ads.
  • Permissions you grant: camera and microphone (to record and go live), photos and files (to upload videos and save to your gallery) and notifications. We use a permission only for the feature it is meant for, and you can turn it off at any time in your phone settings.
  • Biometrics: if you turn on fingerprint or face unlock for app security, your phone checks it on your device. We never receive or store your fingerprint or face data.

Our website

Our website is a simple information site. We do not use advertising cookies on it. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and to keep the site running.

We use your information to provide the app you asked for (to run your account, videos and wallet), because you agreed to it (for example permissions and personalised ads), for our legitimate interests in keeping the app safe and fixing problems, and to follow the law.

3. AI features

When you use captions, translation or AI dubbing, the audio or text of your video is sent to AI service providers for processing, and the result is saved with your video. These providers may process it only to give us the result. We do not use these features to sell your data. AI output can contain mistakes, so please check it before you share.

4. Ads

We show ads through Google AdMob. Ads may use your device advertising ID. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone’s Settings, under Google or Privacy and ads. We do not allow ads that break our rules or the law, and you can report an ad you think is inappropriate.

5. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. What you post publicly (videos, comments, your profile) can be seen by other users, and by people who share or re-post it. We share information only in these cases:

  • Service providers that host and store our data, send notifications and help us run the app. They may use the data only to provide their service to us.
  • Google for sign-in and for ads (Google AdMob).
  • AI providers that process the audio or text of your video to create captions, translations and dubbing.
  • Payment partners that process purchases and withdrawals.
  • The authorities, if the law requires it, if we receive a valid legal request, or to protect the safety of users, especially children.
  • A new owner, if Social Fusion is ever merged or transferred. We will tell you and the same protections will apply.

6. Community safety and content rules

  • We do not allow sexual content involving minors, nudity, graphic violence, hate, harassment, threats, scams, dangerous activities or content that breaks the law. The full rules are in our Terms of Use and Community Guidelines.
  • Report: use the report option on any video, short, live stream, comment or profile. Our team reviews reports and removes content that breaks the rules.
  • Block: you can block any account so they cannot interact with you.
  • We may remove content, limit features or suspend accounts that break the rules. Repeat or serious violations lead to a permanent ban.
  • Child safety is our top priority. We remove content that endangers children and report it to the authorities where the law requires.
  • Copyright: post only content you have the right to use. If your work was uploaded without permission, email mohmmadsarfraz57@gmail.com with the link to the content and proof that you own it, and we will review it and remove it where appropriate.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile, and delete your own videos, comments and stories in the app.
  • Turn off camera, microphone, storage and notification permissions in your phone settings at any time.
  • Reset your advertising ID or turn off personalised ads in your phone settings.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to limit how we use it, or to object to certain uses, by emailing us from the email address linked to your account. We will verify it is you and reply within a reasonable time, normally within 30 days.
  • Delete your account and data, as described below.
  • If you think we handled your data wrongly, contact us first. You may also complain to the data protection authority in your country.

9. Delete your account and data

You can ask us to delete your account and the data we collected about you in either of these ways:

  1. In the app: open Profile → Settings → Delete account and confirm.
  2. By email, even if you cannot open the app: email mohmmadsarfraz57@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account with the subject “Delete my account”. We will confirm your request and delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted: your profile, videos, shorts, stories, comments, likes, messages, wallet balance and the personal data linked to your account. What we may keep: limited payment, security and moderation records that the law or fraud prevention requires us to keep. Copies of your public content that other people shared or saved are outside our control.

10. Children

Social Fusion is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you are under the age of majority in your country, you may use the app only with the permission of a parent or guardian. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it. Parents can email us to see or delete their child’s data.

11. Security

We protect your data with encrypted connections, secure storage on your device and access controls on our servers, and we limit who can access personal data. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong password and keep it private. If a data breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

12. Where your data is stored

Our servers and service providers may be in other countries than yours. By using the app, you agree that your data may be processed there, and we take steps to make sure it stays protected as described in this policy.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app. Using the app after a change means you accept it.
