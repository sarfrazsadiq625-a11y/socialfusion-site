Privacy policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Social Fusion (“we”, “us”) is a video platform where people watch and upload videos and shorts, go live, post stories, comment, send gifts and earn from their content. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, how we keep the community safe and how you can delete your data.

Information we collect

  • Account details: username, email address, password (stored in protected form), profile photo, bio and language.
  • Sign-in with Google: if you use Google Sign-In, we receive your name, email address and profile photo from Google.
  • Content you create: videos, shorts, live streams, stories, captions, comments, likes, subscriptions, playlists and messages.
  • Wallet and payouts: coins, gifts, earnings and withdrawal requests, plus the payout details you provide. Payments are handled by our payment partners, and we do not store your card or bank login details.
  • Device and usage data: device model, operating system, app version, IP address, crash reports, what you watch and search for, and how long you watch.
  • Advertising data: your device advertising ID and your interactions with ads.
  • Permissions you grant: camera and microphone (to record and go live), photos and files (to upload videos and save to your gallery) and notifications. You can turn these off in your phone settings at any time.
  • Biometrics: if you turn on fingerprint or face unlock for app security, your phone checks it on your device. We never receive or store your fingerprint or face data.

How we use your information

  • To create your account, play your videos and run live streams, stories and comments.
  • To recommend videos, shorts and creators you may like.
  • To run the wallet, gifts, coins and withdrawals, and to prevent fraud.
  • To show and measure ads.
  • To provide automatic captions, translation and AI dubbing.
  • To review reports, enforce our community rules and stop spam, abuse and copyright infringement.
  • To fix bugs, improve the app and keep it secure.
  • To send notifications you have asked for and important service messages.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. What you post publicly (videos, comments, your profile) can be seen by other users. We share data only with:

  • Service providers that host and store our data and send notifications.
  • Google for sign-in and for ads (Google AdMob).
  • AI providers that process the audio or text of your video to create captions, translations and dubbing.
  • Payment partners that process withdrawals.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it or to protect the safety of our users.

Ads

We show ads through Google AdMob. Ads may use your advertising ID. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your phone’s Settings, under Google or Privacy and ads.

AI features

When you use captions, translation or AI dubbing, the audio or text of your video is sent to AI providers for processing and the results are saved with your video. We do not use these features to sell your data.

Community safety and content rules

  • We do not allow sexual content involving minors, nudity, graphic violence, hate, harassment, threats, scams, dangerous activities or content that breaks the law.
  • Report: use the report option on any video, short, live stream, comment or profile. Our team reviews reports and removes content that breaks the rules.
  • Block: you can block any account so they cannot interact with you.
  • We may remove content, limit features or suspend accounts that break the rules. Repeat or serious violations lead to a permanent ban.
  • Copyright: post only content you have the right to use. If your work was uploaded without permission, email mohmmadsarfraz57@gmail.com with the link to the content and proof that you own it, and we will review it and remove it where appropriate.

How long we keep data

We keep your information while your account is active. After you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal data within 30 days. We may keep limited records longer where the law or fraud prevention requires it, for example payment records.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile and delete your own videos, comments and stories in the app.
  • Turn off camera, microphone, storage and notification permissions in your phone settings.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct it, by emailing us.
  • Delete your account and data, as described below.

Delete your account and data

You can ask us to delete your account and the data we collected about you in either of these ways:

  1. In the app: open Profile → Settings → Delete account and confirm.
  2. By email, even if you cannot open the app: email mohmmadsarfraz57@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account with the subject “Delete my account”. We will confirm your request and delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted: your profile, videos, shorts, stories, comments, likes, messages, wallet balance and the personal data linked to your account. What we may keep: limited payment and security records that the law requires us to keep.

Children

Social Fusion is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We protect your data with encrypted connections, secure storage on your device and access controls on our servers. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong password and keep it private.

Where your data is stored

Our servers and providers may be in other countries than yours. By using the app, you agree that your data may be processed there with the protections described in this policy.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.

Contact

For privacy questions, reports or data requests, email mohmmadsarfraz57@gmail.com.
