Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about using Social Fusion.

What is Social Fusion?

Social Fusion is a video platform where you can watch videos and shorts, post stories, go live, follow creators and send virtual gifts.

Who can use the app?

You must be at least 13 years old. If you are under the age of majority in your country, you need the permission of a parent or guardian.

How do I report a video, comment or user?

Every video, short, live stream, comment and profile has a Report option. You can also block any account. You can also email us the link and the reason, and our team will review it.

Which content is not allowed?

Child endangerment, nudity and sexual content, graphic violence, hate, harassment, threats, scams, dangerous acts and anything illegal. Read the full Terms of Use and Community Guidelines.

How do I delete my account and data?

Open Profile, then Settings, then Delete account. If you cannot open the app, email us from your registered email address with the subject “Delete my account”. See the account deletion steps.

Do you sell my personal information?

No. We never sell your personal information. Read how we collect and protect your data in the Privacy Policy.

Someone posted my work without permission. What can I do?

Email us the link to the content and proof that you own it. We review copyright requests and remove content where appropriate. Details are in the copyright section.

Questions or feedback?

We read every message. Write to us and we’ll get back to you.
